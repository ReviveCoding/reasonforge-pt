# Claim boundaries

| Claim class | What this export supports | What it does not support |
|---|---|---|
| Frozen primary | Observed C2 − C1 +0.008519, interval [-0.006296, +0.025556], +0.03 gate failed, `RETAIN_CONVENTIONAL_4B` | Established RCAC improvement or retrospective promotion |
| Post-final statistics | Crossed seed × example and other labeled sensitivities | Replacement of the frozen CP100 interval or 450 independent examples |
| Routing | Separate seed-held-out and example-group-held-out diagnostics; no robust benefit | A deployed routing policy or RCAC promotion |
| Uncertainty | Association between model-only proxies and verifier correctness | Calibrated real-world or safety probabilities |
| Systems | Measured replay latency/throughput/telemetry and timestamp-integrated estimated energy | Universal hardware benchmark or unmeasured device-active GPU time |
| Human review | A 60-pair blinded packet | Human preference claims while labels are pending |

The public [claim ledger](../evidence/claim_ledger.json) records source artifacts, evidence type, statistical status, scientific role, promotion eligibility, and limitations. It is a curated derivative and does not alter the internal ledger or frozen decision.
